# demo-reel — 5 · Phasing

*Ordered so that the riskiest unknown is answered first and the first useful
output arrives early. All PROPOSED.*

---

## Phase 0 — Measure the render, before building on it *(half a day)*

**Not a feature. A probe.** `video-creator` advances slides with
`setTimeout(duration * 1000)` while MediaRecorder runs in real time, and nobody
has plotted what that produces.

Build a throwaway page — or add a lane to `media-probe`, which already does this
kind of measurement — that:

1. renders a known 10-scene reel with exact intended durations,
2. reports **intended vs actual** total duration,
3. plots **inter-frame intervals** from the recorded file,
4. repeats it with the tab backgrounded.

**Exit criterion:** a number for drift over 90 seconds, and a decision between
(A) monotonic clock, (B) frame-clocked `requestAnimationFrame`, or (C) WebCodecs
`VideoEncoder`, made from that number.

> This project has twice shipped a constant nobody had plotted — the 30-second
> slices and the `p95 × 1.5` scene threshold. Both were cheap to measure and
> expensive to discover. This is the cheap end.

**Ship nothing.** Write the finding down.

## Phase 1 — The document, the frame, and one landscape video *(2–3 days)*

The narrow vertical slice: reel in, video out, no annotations.

- `dr-state`, `dr-schema`, `dr-timing`, `dr-layout`, `dr-compose`, `dr-record`
- `previewFrame({t})` — deterministic, and the thing every later test hangs off
- `generateAudio()` on `core/sg-tts`, with the content-hash cache
- `render({format:'landscape'})` using whatever Phase 0 chose
- `importImages()` so it can be driven with loose PNGs on day one

**Done when:** an agent can write a `reel.json`, call three actions, and get a
watchable landscape video with narration.

**Tests:** `previewFrame` is deterministic (same `t` → identical pixels);
`getTiming()` splits audio across shots and respects the floor; `render` reports
`drift` and it is within the Phase 0 budget.

---

## Phase 2 — Annotations *(2–3 days)*

The layer that makes these videos worth watching.

- `dr-annotate` — one pure function per primitive: `box`, `circle`, `arrow`,
  `highlight`, `blur`, `tagline`, `label`, `pointer`
- `addAnnotation` / `updateAnnotation` / `removeAnnotation`
- `ui-canvas` with draggable handles, so a human can nudge what an agent placed
- The `in` reveal time

**Done when:** the SGit vault reel has callouts on the key field, an arrow to the
push button, and a blur over a customer name — all authored as JSON, none baked.

**Tests:** each primitive renders at 16:9 and 9:16 from the *same* normalised
coordinates and lands in the same place relative to the image; `blur` actually
obscures (sample the pixels, do not trust the call); annotations survive a
round-trip through `reel.json`.

---

## Phase 3 — Shorts, and text that works muted *(2–3 days)*

- `focus` rects and the 9:16 crop in `dr-layout`
- `focus-too-wide` warning with the honest fallback — **never silently shrink the
  frame to a stamp**
- `dr-caption`: wrapping, auto-sizing, safe margins, contrast plate
- `caption-derived` and `caption-overflow` warnings
- `render({format:'shorts'})`

**Done when:** the same document produces both formats, and the vertical one is
readable on a phone with the sound off.

**Tests:** a focus rect wider than 9:16 raises the warning rather than producing a
bad video; a long caption either wraps and fits or warns; captions never overlap
the safe margins.

---

## Phase 3.5 — Clips as shots *(2 days, gated on Phase 0)*

Some things are not stills: a panel docking, a graph settling, a progress bar
finishing. A three-still approximation of motion is worse than either option.

- `clip` + `trim` on a shot; clip duration wins, narration mismatch **warns**
- `dr-compose` draws a video frame at `t` — the determinism/speed tension from
  Decision 9, resolved the way Phase 0 pointed
- `recordClip()` driving a Playwright-recorded context

**Gated on Phase 0** because seeking a video per frame is exactly the case where
a naive renderer falls over, and WebCodecs is exactly what fixes it.

**Done when:** a reel mixes annotated stills and a 4-second clip without the
audio drifting from either.

---

## Phase 4 — Import from narrated-review *(1–2 days)*

- `importSession()` for a zip, a handover bundle, or a `sg-vfs` session id
- The documented mapping, with `marks` **dropped** and `textSource:'raw'`
  captures **skipped with a reason**
- `mergeScenes` / `splitScene`, because the natural edit after import is
  "these three captures are one point"

**Done when:** a narrated walkthrough recorded in the morning is a published reel
by lunchtime, with the narration already written.

**Tests:** an uncorrected transcript is skipped and reported, not promoted;
`marks` never reach `narration`; merge/split are inverses.

---

## Phase 5 — Publish *(1 day)*

- `downloadBundle()` in the shared folder shape
- Hand off to `youtube-upload` / `video-publisher` (both shipped)
- Save the reel to an sgit vault, so a reel is versioned like everything else
- A poster frame via `exportFrames()`

---

## Deliberately later

- **Word-level caption sync.** Ruled out in the memo, and it is the difference
  between a week and a month.
- **Transitions and motion.** Cuts only in v1. A Ken Burns pan is tempting and is
  also how a simple renderer becomes a compositor.
- **Music beds and ducking.**
- **Multi-language.** Kokoro has voices; the document has one `narration` field.
  Making it `narration: {en, pt}` later is additive — do not pre-build it.
- **Brand theming beyond one dark theme.**

---

## Risks, honestly

| Risk | Why it matters | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **Render pacing** | A 90-second video whose audio slips from its captions is unpublishable, and it fails silently | Phase 0 measures it; `drift` is reported on every render forever after |
| **9:16 is genuinely lossy** | Some screenshots cannot be cropped to vertical without losing the point | Warn and fall back visibly; never pretend |
| **Kokoro on a long reel** | 20 scenes of TTS could be slow enough to break the ten-minute goal | The 8-worker pool is already parallel; cache by content hash; measure in Phase 1 |
| **Screenshot legibility** | A 1920×1080 screenshot scaled into 1080×1920 makes 12px UI text unreadable | The `focus` rect exists precisely for this — crop and scale up rather than fit the whole frame |
| **MediaRecorder codec variance** | WebM vs MP4 differs by browser; YouTube is fine, other places are not | `getBestMimeType()` already exists in `core/sg-video-recorder`; state the output format in the bundle |
| **Scope creep into a video editor** | The nearest failure mode; `sg-video-editor` already exists and this is not it | The non-goals in part 1 are load-bearing. This tool *generates*; it does not *edit* footage |

## What I could not verify while writing this

- **`video-creator` has not been run here.** Its pipeline was read, not executed.
  Everything above about its behaviour comes from the source
  (`api/video-pipeline.js`), which is why Phase 0 measures rather than assumes.
- **Kokoro TTS timings are unmeasured** in this container.
- The **ten-minute goal is an aspiration**, not a measurement. It should be timed
  end to end on the first real vault reel and reported.
